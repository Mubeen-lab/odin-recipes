Burger Recipe

Description

A classic hamburger with a juicy patty and fresh toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef and drain the excess fat.
  2. Add the onion and garlic to the pan and cook until softened.
  3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper.
  4. Simmer the sauce for at least 20 minutes.
