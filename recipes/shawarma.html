Shawarma Recipe

Description

A delicious Middle Eastern wrap filled with spiced meat and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lamb or chicken, diced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon coriander
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. In a large pan, heat some oil and sauté the onion until translucent.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  3. Add the meat and cook until browned.
  4. Stir in the spices and cook for a few more minutes.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
